# Learning-DSA
LEVEL 1 — C++ Fundamentals
Q1. Personal Information
Write a C++ program that takes your Name, Age, and College name as input and prints them in a properly
formatted output.
Q2. Rectangle
Take the length and width of a rectangle as input and calculate Area and Perimeter.
Q3. Temperature Conversion
Take temperature in Celsius and convert it into Fahrenheit.
Q4. Type Casting
Take two integers a and b. Calculate their average in such a way that the result can contain decimal
values. Example: Input: 5 2 ® Output: 3.5.
Q5. Operators
Take two integers and print Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, and Remainder.
Q6. Swap Two Numbers
Take two numbers and swap their values. Example: Input: 10 20 ® Output: 20 10.
Q7. Even or Odd
Take an integer and determine whether it is Even or Odd.
Q8. Positive, Negative or Zero
Take an integer and determine whether it is Positive, Negative, or Zero.
Q9. Maximum of Two Numbers
Take two integers and print the greater number.
Q10. Maximum of Three Numbers
Take three integers and print the largest number.
