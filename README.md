# Learning-DSA
LEVEL 1 — C++ Fundamentals <br>
Q1. Personal Information<br>
Write a C++ program that takes your Name, Age, and College name as input and prints them in a properly<br>
formatted output.<br>
Q2. Rectangle<br>
Take the length and width of a rectangle as input and calculate Area and Perimeter.<br>
Q3. Temperature Conversion<br>
Take temperature in Celsius and convert it into Fahrenheit.<br>
Q4. Type Casting<br>
Take two integers a and b. Calculate their average in such a way that the result can contain decimal<br>
values. Example: Input: 5 2 ® Output: 3.5.<br>
Q5. Operators<br>
Take two integers and print Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, and Remainder.<br>
Q6. Swap Two Numbers<br>
Take two numbers and swap their values. Example: Input: 10 20 ® Output: 20 10.<br>
Q7. Even or Odd<br>
Take an integer and determine whether it is Even or Odd.<br>
Q8. Positive, Negative or Zero<br>
Take an integer and determine whether it is Positive, Negative, or Zero.<br>
Q9. Maximum of Two Numbers<br>
Take two integers and print the greater number.<br>
Q10. Maximum of Three Numbers<br>
Take three integers and print the largest number.<br>
